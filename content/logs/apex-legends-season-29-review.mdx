## Back Again

Three seasons in a row now. Whatever Respawn is doing to keep this game feeling alive this deep into its lifespan, it's working. Season 29 doesn't reinvent anything — it refines the ranked changes from 27 and 28 and layers in a new legend kit that actually shifts squad compositions in ranked lobbies.

> CHAMPION SQUAD: Eliminated. Cause of death: Coil. Again.

## What Changed

The new legend's kit rewards aggressive third-partying in a way that, frankly, plays right into a certain squadmate's hands. Ranked has never felt more punishing for passive play, which is either a great change or a personal attack depending on who's calling shots that game.

> BANGALORE: Smoke deployed. Vision: zero. Plan: unclear. Again.

## The Constant

Coil is still doing it. The third-party from the building nobody agreed to push. The split at ring closure that somehow works out. Three seasons of documented evidence and the defense remains the same: it's never his fault, except it always is.

## Final Verdict

*Always Coil's fault.* Another strong season for a battle royale that refuses to coast, held together by the same squad chaos as ever.
